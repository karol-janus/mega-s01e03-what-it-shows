Parser: the shared parser reads month first

Day-first tenants sending dates through the app or email, and through booking-v2 forms, hit the shared month-first parser. A booking for 7 April becomes 4 July. See R-41200 (harbour.dental, app). Correct the parser to use the tenant’s date order and test ambiguous day/month inputs, unambiguous dates, and month-first tenants as controls.

Hint: booking-v3 advertises the wrong order

For day-first tenants, booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY, although its form reads the input in the tenant’s day-first order. People follow the hint and their dates flip. See R-41230 (copper.studio, form). Fix the hint to match the form’s actual locale-aware interpretation; changing the shared parser alone does not fix this path. Test alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour with the form, then confirm their app/email paths still belong to the parser issue.

Reports arriving and reports being merged are different events

The received series starts in April. The large July merge bar records desk processing of an existing backlog; it is not a July outbreak. Reports keep arriving after that merge and through October. A known label and a queued fix do not show that the incident stopped.
